Research Interests

  • Plasma Physics and magnetized plasmas.
  • Coherent and Terahertz Radiation Generation (THz plasmons, free electron lasers, surface magnetoplasmons).
  • Renewable Energy Technologies and Energy Studies (fuel cells, hybrid energy systems, rural electrification).
  • Photonics & Optics (plasma-cladded fibers, dispersion in uniaxial crystalline fibers).
  • Generative AI and Machine Learning applications to physics and energy modeling (professional certificate, IIT Kanpur 2025).

Major Research Themes

Terahertz Radiation and Plasma-Based Sources

  • Beat excitation of THz radiation in semiconductor slabs and magnetized plasma channels.
  • Excitation of THz plasmons eigenmodes in guiding structures driven by electron beams.
  • Free Electron Laser configurations with tapered magnetic fields for efficient THz generation.

Hybrid Renewable Energy Systems

  • Design and demonstration of 1.5 MW Integrated Dairy & Smart Hybrid Energy Systems for rural India.
  • Photovoltaic-based electrification of hostels and integration with other energy sources.
  • Fuel cell performance, contamination, and stabilization strategies.

Fundamental Theoretical Work

  • Unified treatment of atomic, gravitational, and electrical energy from a theoretical physics viewpoint.
  • Philosophical and mathematical investigations such as “GOD doesn’t play dice” and prime numbers with alphabets.
  • Reformulations of electromagnetic theory using minimal units (second and meter).
